About me

Hi, I’m Aris.

I’m a graphic designer and visual storyteller from Pahang, Malaysia. I design posters, apparel, social content and concept art inspired by anime, indie games and Disney films, and I shoot and edit video when a story needs to move.

I care about the details — the type, the rhythm, the colour — so that every piece feels intentional and leaves an impression long after the first glance.

Beyond design

Rhythm on and off the page.

Outside the studio you’ll find me on stage with a caklempong ensemble — a traditional Malay gong-chime orchestra. Playing in a group taught me timing, listening, and how many small parts come together into one performance.

“Good design, like good music, is all about rhythm.”

Let’s build something memorable.
